## The four problems (one card each: big number, one line, one habit)

| Card | Big number | One line | Desk habit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 · Rejected tenders | 78% vs 90 to 94% [19, 20] | Rejected loads cost more as they "move down routing guides" [23]. | Book early: "Give carriers 48 to 72 hours of lead time." [4] |
| 02 · Short notice | "Short-lead tenders are the loads asset carriers reject first" [5] | Bulk lead time moved from "24 to 72 hours to 7 to 10 days" [3]. | Plan bulk and heated equipment a week ahead. |
| 03 · Waiting at the dock | 39.3% of stops [27] | $3.6B direct, $11.5B productivity lost to detention in 2023, industry-wide [28]. Poor shipping hygiene "can swing rate evaluations by 10% or more" [24]. | Clean appointments, paperwork ready, track detention by facility [26]. |

## Calculator: "What rejections cost your network"
Inputs (all typed by the reader; defaults are blank or clearly labeled "example input, replace with yours"):
- Loads per week
- Your first-tender acceptance rate (%) (reference ticks on the slider: 78% and 90 to 94% [19, 20])
- Target acceptance rate (%)
- Extra cost per rejected load ($): what a backup or spot carrier costs above the primary

Outputs:
- Rejected loads per week now = loads × (1 − acceptance)
- Extra cost per week now = rejected loads × extra cost
- Extra cost per year = weekly × 52
- Money back at target = (target − current acceptance) × loads × extra cost × 52

Small print: `Uses your inputs only. Uber Freight does not publish its cost per rejected load.`
